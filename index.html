Japanese Diophantine Factorizer

Give a semiprime n = p·q. The solver searches the carry equations for a perfect base B where both carries vanish, then draws p × q as Japanese stick multiplication in that base.

Already know the factors? Enter p and q directly

Runs entirely in your browser with exact BigInt arithmetic. Practical up to about 72 bit keys. The search is ratio dependent: it finds a perfect base when q/p sits near a low order Farey fraction.

The carry landscape

Column-0 carry ratio c₀/B across the active window. Vermillion marks are zero-carry bases. In the three-digit window (√(n/2) < B ≤ √n, leading digit 1) they form one interval hugging p — no comb needed; each of them solves n exactly. In the classic cube-root window they are the comb teeth predicted by the continued fraction of q/p. Click anywhere to inspect that base.

Exact solve at the current B (three-digit window)

With n = B² + d₁·B + d₀ and both carries zero, the remainders g₁, g₂ are the roots of x² − d₁·x + d₀ = 0. Everything below is computed from n and B alone, then self-checked.

Method: perfect-base Diophantine factorization, carry equations E0–E2 solved exactly. Research code, toy key sizes only. Real RSA keys keep q/p away from low order fractions, which closes this surface completely.
